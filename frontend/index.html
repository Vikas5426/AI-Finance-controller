Standard Operating Procedures (SOP Engine)
Declarative 5-tier financial reconciliation & exception policies.
SOP-01: Deterministic Exact Matching P0 Tier

1:1 reference key matching (Invoice ID, Payment ID, UTR) where amounts match to 0 paise residual.

Tolerance: ₹0.00 | Action: Auto-Commit to Ledger
SOP-02: Period Cutoff Timing Boundary P1 Tier

Captures occurring within 15 minutes of monthly boundary (23:59:59 IST) where bank credit settles under standard T+2 window.

Tolerance: T+2 SLA | Action: Accrue Account 1290 (In-Transit)
SOP-03: Reversal & Chargeback Netting P2 Tier

Refunds or chargeback debits linked to original settled transaction reference.

Tolerance: Full/Partial Netting | Action: Offset Contra-Revenue
SOP-04: MDR Fee & GST Deduction Netting P3 Tier

Gateway deposits booked net of Merchant Discount Rate (2.0% / 1.5% Enterprise) + 18% GST.

Formula: Gross - MDR - (MDR * 0.18) | Action: Debit Acc 5010
SOP-05: Missing Settlement & UTR Tracing P4 Tier

Gateway captures exceeding 48-hour clearing SLA without matching bank statement deposit credit.

Threshold: >48 Hrs | Action: Hold for Maker-Checker & Trace UTR
Financial Accounting Manual & ITGC Controls
GAAP, ASC 606, SOX-404 & Dual-Control Governance.
1. ASC 606 / Ind AS 115 Revenue Recognition

Gross transaction amounts are recognized when performance obligation is fulfilled at capture. Processing fees are presented as operating expense (Debit 5010) rather than revenue reduction.

2. SOX-404 Internal Controls over Financial Reporting

Segregation of duties enforces that no analyst (Maker) can approve or post their own proposed adjustment vouchers. All approvals require dual-control sign-off by a designated Controller (Checker).

✔ Automated dual-control authorization check active
3. Cryptographic SHA-256 Ledger Immutability

Each batch lifecycle event (Ingestion, Validation, Hungarian Matching, Exception Triage, Proposal Approval) produces a SHA-256 block cryptographically linked to the Genesis block, preventing unauthorized modifications.

4. Materiality & Escalation Matrix
• Tier 1 (< ₹500): Auto-remediated with deterministic math verification.
• Tier 2 (₹500 – ₹25,000): Analyst (Maker) proposal + Automated rule check.
• Tier 3 (₹25,000 – ₹1,00,000): Mandatory Controller (Checker) dual sign-off.
• Tier 4 (> ₹1,00,000): Executive CFO / Treasury Director authorization.
